01 / ABOUT

Good software starts
with thoughtful engineering.

I’m Konstantinos, a mobile software engineer based in Athens, Greece. I currently work as an Android Engineer at Alpha Bank, with previous experience at Almende B.V., StandBy Solutions B.V. and Softship GmbH.

I focus on mobile experiences from interface to architecture, combining a strong foundation in native Android and Kotlin with cross-platform development. My toolkit includes Jetpack Compose and Kotlin Multiplatform, with MVVM and Clean Architecture keeping software maintainable. My public projects also explore Flutter and React Native.
